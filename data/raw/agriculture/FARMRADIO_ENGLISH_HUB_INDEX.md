Full article index from `scripts.farmradio.fm/topic/agriculture/`, all 94 pages
(940 articles), crawled 2026-07-15. Regenerate with the same pagination pattern in
`fetch_farmradio.py`'s docstring; re-run is safe (fetchlib caches responses).

~21 rows have been promoted into `agriculture_psas.csv` from this hub across sessions
(see `SOURCES.md` for which). This session triaged the remainder by keyword scoring
(titles/intros containing "how to," "manage," "control," disease/pest names, numbers
with units, etc.) and fetched+scored the top 80 candidates; roughly 858 titles have not
even been fetched yet. Large real lead for a future pass — the triage script and
scoring method are reusable, not just this static list.

Unlike the Swahili-locale "kilimo" hub (fully mined via `_candidates_farmradio.csv`),
most of these English articles have no Kiswahili hreflang pair (many pair to French
instead) and would need team-translation, not a source-published parallel pair.
